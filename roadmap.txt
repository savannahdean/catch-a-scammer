TABLES NEEDED

cases — the case file itself: case code, victim summary, briefing text, whether it's solved, and which entity/campaign is the correct answer.

domains — malicious or related domain names, with a status (malicious, benign, unknown) and first/last seen dates.

ip_addresses — IP addresses that domains resolve to, including which hosting provider and country/region they belong to.

hosting_providers — companies that host IPs, just an id and a name.

malware — malware families, with a type, first observed date, optional hash, and source.

campaigns — named campaigns that group malware and threat actors together.

threat_actors — the people/groups behind everything, identified by an alias, with notes.

web_articles — fake blog/news snippets the WebSearch app shows, each pointing at a domain or threat actor.

evidence — items the player collects during the case, tied to a specific case.

entity_relationships — the glue table. It just says "this thing connects to that thing" for every pair above 
(domain to IP, IP to hosting provider, malware to campaign, and so on), so you don't need a separate table for every possible pairing.
